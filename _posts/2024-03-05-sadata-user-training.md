The workshop, organised as part of SPC's PROJECT Governance, provided practical training on using [Sadata](https://sadata.ws) to manage and track Samoa's human rights, SDG and national development commitments.

Alongside the training, Sadata was updated to replace the previous national development plan with Samoa's new [Pathway for the Development of Samoa](https://www.mof.gov.ws/publications/economic-policy-planning/pathway-for-the-development-of-samoa), involving both code updates and a joint effort to prepare and update the database content.
